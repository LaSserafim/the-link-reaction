Arrival at the Mitochondrion

The molecular bridge between glycolysis and the Krebs cycle.

📍 WHERE

Cytosol bordering the porous outer mitochondrial membrane.

⚡ WHAT

Pyruvate (3C) from glycolysis moves toward the mitochondrion to begin aerobic respiration.

🔬 HOW

Pyruvate diffuses passively across the permeable outer mitochondrial membrane.

🔗 WHY & CONNECTIONS

Links cytosolic glycolysis to the matrix-based Krebs cycle to access high-yield aerobic ATP production.

Biological Role in Respiration

End-product of glycolysis. Must be actively transported into the mitochondrial matrix to undergo oxidative decarboxylation.

Fate in Link Reaction

Decarboxylated by the PDC to yield CO₂ and an acetyl fragment which is transferred to CoA.

Key Syllabus Note: Glycolysis produces 2 molecules of pyruvate per glucose molecule; therefore, this entire reaction operates twice per glucose.
